Communication is the single most important function in our business. Deadlines are commitments, not targets. The standards below define what is expected, who owns it, and when it is due — every week, on time, without reminder.

01

Bid Procurement

Owner: Justin Vostad — currently assisting the Director

Subcontractor Bids Returned Within 10 business days
OwnerJustin Vostad Clock StartsDate plan is sent for bidding Clock / DeadlineWithin 10 business days

From the date a plan is sent to Justin for bidding, all subcontractor bids are due back within ten (10) business days — 2–3 bids for each applicable cost code. Bids go out promptly (a plan received Friday should be out the following Monday) so the full window is used for collecting, not staging.

Bids Coded & Organized Within 3 business days of return
OwnerJustin Vostad Clock StartsDay bids are returned Clock / DeadlineWithin 3 business days of return

Once bids are back, Justin has three (3) additional business days to code them to the correct cost codes and organize them in our filing system and Buildertrend, ready for the Director to review. Example: plan sent Friday → out Monday → bids back by the Friday 10 working days later → coded and organized by the following Wednesday.

Site Logs Same day as each visit
OwnerSuper on each job FrequencySF metro: 3×/week per job
Outside metro: 1×/week per job Clock / DeadlineSame day as each visit

A log entry is required at least three times per week for each active job inside the Sioux Falls metro, and at least once per week for each job outside the metro. Note crews on site, trades present, work completed, deliveries, weather delays, and any issue affecting schedule. Cadence reflects travel time between spread-out jobs — it is a floor, not a cap.

Site Photos Same day, with the site log
OwnerSuper on each job FrequencySF metro: 3×/week per job
Outside metro: 1×/week per job Clock / DeadlineSame day, with the site log

Photo coverage of all active work areas on the same cadence as the site logs — 3×/week per job inside the metro, 1×/week outside — tied to the correct job in Buildertrend. Capture progress, any deficiency or damage, and completed phases. Photos protect us on warranty, billing, and dispute.

2-Week Look-Ahead Friday, 5:00 PM — before leaving
OwnerSuper on each job FrequencyEvery Friday Clock / DeadlineFriday, 5:00 PM — before leaving

A forward two-week schedule submitted before end of business every Friday. Going forward this is completed in the office on Fridays. It must show trade sequencing, expected starts/completions, material needs, and any anticipated gaps so coordination happens before the week starts — not during it.

03

Client Communication — PM on Job

Responsible PM for each project

Bi-Weekly Project Update Friday, 5:00 PM — before leaving
OwnerPM on Job FrequencyBi-weekly — Group A or Group B Friday Clock / DeadlineFriday, 5:00 PM — before leaving

Jobs are split equally into Group A and Group B. Group A goes out one Friday, Group B the next, alternating, so each project receives a written update every two weeks. Cover progress, what's planned next, any decisions or selections needed, and any schedule change. Distribute to all interested parties — client, investor, realtor, the Director (CC), and any additional parties on internal projects. The Director is CC'd on every update as confirmation it went out.

Weekly Site Visit Within each work week
OwnerPM on Job FrequencyAt least 1× per week, per site Clock / DeadlineWithin each work week

The responsible PM physically visits every one of their active sites at least once per week. Verify work against schedule and plan, confirm quality, and catch issues the daily logs don't surface. Buildertrend is not a substitute for being on site.

Notes

  • Bid procurement — the two milestones are the governing commitments: bids returned within 10 business days of the plan being sent, and coded/organized within 3 business days of bids being returned. Each cost code carries 2–3 bids where applicable.
  • Project update groups — jobs are divided equally into Group A and Group B; the groups alternate Fridays so every project is updated on a two-week cycle. Distribution list per update: client, investor, realtor, Director (CC), plus any additional interested parties on internal projects.
  • Friday deadlines — “before they leave” means submitted by 5:00 PM Friday. The 2-week look-ahead is completed in the office on Fridays going forward.
  • Warranty timeline — language is quoted directly from the Van Buskirk Homes warranty agreement: claims acknowledged within three (3) business days, inspection/repair date provided within ten (10) business days, except emergencies per Section 5.5.
  • Business days — exclude weekends and recognized company holidays. The clock starts on receipt of the written claim.
  • Accountability — these standards are the reference for responsibilities assigned in the RACI Matrix. “Owner” is the role accountable for the standard being met on time.
