Governing Notes

  • PM on Job — the role column for the lead PM assigned to a given project (Sydney for custom homes; Justin for twinhomes, townhomes & Hitchcock Park). A 3rd PM, or reassignment of design or warranty, fills the corresponding role column without restructuring the matrix. The named PM columns carry only cross-job duties performed regardless of whose job it is.
  • Approval Thresholds — PMs are A/R for buyouts, POs, change orders, and allowance overages at or under the estimated / customer-approved amount. Any amount exceeding estimate, or any allowance overage not already approved by the customer, requires Director of Operations approval.
  • Plan Development — R may be delegated to an external design / drafting vendor; the Director of Operations remains Accountable and coordinates plans not drawn in-house.
  • Marketing Coordination — execution performed by the external marketing team (R); the Director of Operations coordinates and remains Accountable.
  • Change Orders — routed PM → Dallas (front half) or PM → Quentin (back half). PM on Job is Accountable for the change-order process; dollar escalations follow the Approval Threshold note above.
  • Lien Waivers — Kara issues waivers via Buildertrend prior to payment; if a sub is unresponsive, the PM on Job is Accountable for resolution before funds are released.
  • Final Walkthrough — Justin conducts the walkthrough as a cross-job function with Quentin on site to facilitate pre-closing completion; Brandt attends as client advocate and reports notes to the Director, who gives final approval.
  • Business Development & Leadership Meeting — Accountability for these standing meetings rests with the Chief Operating Officer, a role not represented as a column on this matrix.
